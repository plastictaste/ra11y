# 2.3.1 Three Flashes or Below Threshold

**Level:** A | **Standard:** WCAG 2.2
**Spec:** <https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/#three-flashes-or-below-threshold>

## Why manual

Flashing content is a runtime behavior. Static analysis can flag CSS animations, `<canvas>` elements, video embeds, and GIF images that may flash, but cannot measure actual flash frequency, luminance transitions, or whether content falls below the general flash or red-flash thresholds without executing the content.

## What to verify

> "Web pages do not contain anything that flashes more than three times in any one second period, or the flash is below the general flash and red flash thresholds."

Note (normative): Since content failing this criterion can interfere with overall page usability, all content on the web page must satisfy this criterion. This relates to Conformance Requirement 5: Non-Interference.

Definitions:
- **General flash threshold:** a pair of opposing changes in relative luminance ≥ 10% and saturating within 1/10 second in an area > 25% of 10° visual field at typical viewing distance.
- **Red flash:** any pair of opposing transitions involving a saturated red.
- A "flash" is a pair of opposing changes (increase then decrease, or vice versa) in relative luminance.

## Common failure modes

- An animated GIF or CSS `@keyframes` animation toggles high-contrast colors rapidly (e.g., strobe effect).
- A video embed contains a scene with rapid high-luminance flashes (concert lighting, explosion effects).
- A loading spinner or progress animation flashes at a rate above 3Hz.
- A `<canvas>`-based game or visualization produces rapid color changes.
- Auto-playing video ads or hero banners with strobe-like transitions.

## Attestation guidance

To attach evidence to an `attest` call (`ra11y-kb://criteria/wcag22/2.3.1`):

- Enumerate all animation sources: CSS animations, `<video>`, `<canvas>`, animated GIFs, SVG animations.
- For any animation with rapid color/luminance changes, measure flash frequency (tool: PEAT — Photosensitive Epilepsy Analysis Tool).
- Confirm flashing content either: (a) does not flash more than 3 times/second, or (b) passes the general flash and red-flash thresholds.
- Document any auto-playing video or canvas content.

Evidence: list of animation sources, flash measurement results (frequency, threshold compliance), tool used.
